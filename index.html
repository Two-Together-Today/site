A premarital course for two

Nine sessions (about 9½ hours total) of lessons, conversations, and activities for engaged couples. Work through them together, one or two a week. Your progress saves in this browser only.

Important — Texas fee reduction. Under Texas Family Code §2.013, couples who complete an approved 8-hour premarital education course within 12 months before applying can get the marriage license fee reduced (currently $60) and the 72-hour waiting period waived. Collin County’s Clerk generally requires a completion certificate from a provider registered with the state’s Twogether in Texas program. A self-guided website cannot issue that certificate by itself. To use this curriculum for the discount, have it delivered or verified by a registered provider (licensed counselor, therapist, social worker, psychologist, or qualified clergy) who signs your certificate. Confirm current rules at twogetherintexas.com and with the Collin County Clerk before you start.

This course is education, not therapy. If either of you feels unsafe, see Session 9 for resources.
